A neighborhood garden uses three raised beds to compare watering schedules. Bed 1
receives water every morning, while Bed 2 is watered on alternate days. Bed 3 follows a
weekly schedule. The notebook contains temperature readings such as 18.6 C and short
observations about the leaves. These values are fictional, not measurements from a real
garden. At the end of each week, volunteers count the seedlings and check the hose
connections. Labels should remain readable after light rain. Store spare markers in the
covered box, and write the next inspection date beside each bed number.
